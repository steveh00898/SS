    How tiers are compared

    Each numeric specification is compared independently within its product category, using the full saved catalog. Resistance levels and motorized speed levels are compared separately. Numeric tiers use distinct advertised values: the upper third is green, the lower third is grey, and the middle is unchanged. Fewer than three distinct values remain unranked. Search, sorting and comparison do not change the colors.

    Treadmill incline tiers compare adjustability: incline + decline is highest, automatic incline is mid, and fixed or manually adjusted incline is lowest. Other motion systems and user-powered speed/drive are unranked. Quoted motor HP is not a standardized continuous-duty rating.

    Elliptical numeric stride lengths are ranked within Ellipticals. Resistance technologies and descriptive rower motion labels stay unranked because they are not directly comparable performance measures.

    Dimensions are copied from your master portfolio. Only treadmill workout dimensions split into length × width; overall or folded elliptical and rower dimensions are not workout-pad dimensions. “-” means information has not been provided. Green indicates a higher specification band, not an overall product recommendation.
